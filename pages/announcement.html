🎉 College Fest Registration Started!

Registration for the College Fest has officially started. Students can register for their favorite cultural, technical, and sports events through the Registration page. Participants are requested to complete the registration process on time and provide the correct details while registering. Make sure to select the events you are interested in before the registration deadline.

📅 Registration Status: Open

🎤 Cultural Events Registration

Students interested in Dance, Singing, Drama, and Fashion Show can register now. These cultural events provide students with an opportunity to showcase their creativity, confidence, and talent. Participants should prepare their performances in advance and follow the instructions provided by the event coordinators.

🎭 Events: Dance | Singing | Drama | Fashion Show

💻 Technical Events

Registration is open for Coding Contest and Quiz Competition. Students can participate and test their technical knowledge, problem-solving skills, logical thinking, and creativity. Participants should report to the assigned venue on time and carry any required materials or college ID card.

💻 Events: Coding Contest | Quiz Competition

📸 Photography & Sports Events

Students can also participate in Photography and Sports Events. The Photography event gives students an opportunity to capture creative and memorable moments during the fest. Sports events will encourage teamwork, fitness, discipline, and healthy competition among students. Interested students are encouraged to register before the deadline.

🏅 Events: Photography | Sports Events
