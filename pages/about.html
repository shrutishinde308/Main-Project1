About the Fest

The College Fest is the biggest annual event organized by our college. It provides a platform for students to showcase their talents, creativity, leadership, and teamwork through various cultural, technical, and sports activities.

Objectives

  1. Encourage student participation.
  2. Develop leadership skills.
  3. Promote creativity and innovation.
  4. Improve teamwork and communication.
  5. Create memorable experiences.

Events Included

  • Dance Competition
  • Singing Competition
  • Drama
  • Fashion Show
  • Coding Contest
  • Quiz Competition
  • Photography
  • Sports Events

Our Vision

To organize a successful college festival that inspires students, encourages innovation, and builds confidence while promoting friendship and unity among all participants.
